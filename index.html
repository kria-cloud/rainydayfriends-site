About

Hi, I’m Eva

I make cozy, cute-not-scary coloring pages from my home in Iceland, where rainy days are part of everyday life. Rainy Day Friends began with a simple idea: printable pages that turn a grey afternoon into something fun to do together.

Every page is designed with care, from the soft rounded lines to the tiny raindrop heart that hides on each one.

Is this a physical book?

No, these are digital downloads. After checkout you get the PDF files straight away and print them at home or at a print shop.

Which paper size do I get?

Both. Every book comes as US Letter and A4, so it prints perfectly wherever you are.

Can I print the pages more than once?

Yes, as often as you like for your own family or your classroom. Please don’t share or resell the files.

What paper and crayons work best?

Plain printer paper is great for crayons and colored pencils. For markers or watercolor, use thicker paper or cardstock.

How do payments work?

Checkout is handled securely by Payhip. You can pay with card or PayPal, and the download link arrives by email too.
